## 💡 What is AyushCare AI?

AyushCare AI simplifies the healthcare journey by collecting patient information before consultation and presenting it to doctors in a structured format.

### 🔄 Workflow

```text
Patient
  ↓
Registration & Consent
  ↓
Health Assessment
  ↓
AI-Assisted Triage
  ↓
AYUSH Assessment
  ↓
Case Summary
  ↓
Doctor OPD
  ↓
Prescription & Treatment
  ↓
Follow-up
```

## 🧠 AI + AYUSH

The platform combines AI-assisted healthcare workflows with AYUSH-oriented information such as:

- **Prakriti**
- **Agni**
- **Koshtha**
- **Jihwa Status**
- Symptoms & severity
- Pathya / Apathya
- Anupana
- AYUSH treatment recommendations

> AI results are designed to support healthcare professionals and do not replace medical judgment.
